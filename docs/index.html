Profile

My name is Federico Mora Rocha. I am an assistant professor of computer science at the University of Waterloo and a faculty affiliate at the Vector Institute. Previously, I was an applied scientist at Amazon Web Services and a Ph.D. student at UC Berkeley.

I study automated reasoning, programming language theory, and neuro-symbolic artificial intelligence. For more information about my work, see my curriculum vitae.
