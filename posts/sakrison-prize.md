title: Won the David J. Sakrison Memorial Prize
date: 04/2026
---

I won the David J. Sakrison Memorial Prize, which is awarded annually to UC
Berkeley EECS students "who have completed what is deemed by a faculty committee to
be a truly outstanding piece of research." The prize recognized my
[dissertation](https://federico.morarocha.ca/thesis/). 

A full description of the award and a list of past winners is available on [the
award website](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Students/Awards/17/).
